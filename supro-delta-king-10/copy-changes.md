  linked as "Download the Manual" on https://www.suprousa.com/amplifiers/delta-king-10/
  and as "Delta King 10" on https://www.suprousa.com/support/product-manuals/,
  both pointing at https://www.suprousa.com/uploads/supro/2021/01/1820_DK10_Manual.pdf.

  photo, so no credit needed). The PDF has no product photo.

## Dropped

- The Contents page (p.3): the side nav and mobile TOC replace it. Its entry
  "3 Supro Warranty Policy" disagrees with the heading "3. SUPRO LIMITED
  WARRANTY"; the page follows the heading.
- Page numbers; the back cover (its `suprousa.com` and © line are in the footer).

## Left as printed, flagged

- Troubleshooting says to check "that the speaker is plugged into the speaker
  jack (not the footswitch jack)", but the rear-panel drawing shows only the
  Boost/Drive FTSW and Line Out jacks.
- "No sound and the lights are all OFF": the panel has one pilot light.
- Manufacturer is "DAG Audio LLC" while the © line names "Bond Audio LLC".

## Layout borrowed from the BOSS RC-5 page (at the owner's request)

- WARNING / CAUTION run-ins set as badges; their colons dropped.
- "Steps To File for Warranty Coverage": the two printed lines are a numbered list (numbers added).
- Troubleshooting cases in ruled boxes; chapter and section headings in block-and-bar treatments.
