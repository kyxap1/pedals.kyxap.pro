## Conflicts between sources, left as printed

- Name: the User Guide says "ZIO Bass", the card says "Bass ZIO".
- Higher voltage: the User Guide says "Please do NOT use 12V, 18V, etc. power supplies, or risk damaging the pedal and voiding the warranty"; the card says "Using a higher voltage than 9V is not recommended."
- GAIN trimmer: "small Phillips head screwdriver" (User Guide) against "small screwdriver" (card).
- GRIT: "vintage bass amp transformer-like distortion" (User Guide) against "mild distortion" (card).
- Input level: Specifications give "Maximum input headroom: +/-10V" (20 V peak-to-peak); the FAQ says the inputs "can handle up to 8.76 dBu or 6.0 Volts peak-to-peak".
- Weight: "0.45kg / 1lbs", kept as printed.

## Template leftovers kept as printed

- Troubleshooting, Ground loop with amp: "…as your guitar amplifier" (the pedal is for bass).
- The panel jack is labelled OUTPUT; the User Guide heading says "Main OUTPUT" and the card "MAIN OUTPUT".
