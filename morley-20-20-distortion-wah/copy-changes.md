## Conflicts left as printed

- Dimensions: the manual gives 6.75" L x 4.5" W x 2.75" H; the product page
  gives 6.85″ x 4.5″ x 2.5″ (L x W x H).
- Power: the manual says ONLY Morley's regulated adapter should be used; the
  product page says "Compatible with most 9V adapters".

## Added beyond the manual

- `further-notes` section, marked as not from the manual: buffered bypass
  (Equipboard, Morley's "Buffers vs. True Bypass" article), the fixed
  wah → distortion order and the redesigned PDW circuit (Thomann, rockboard),
  and three checks for a tone change with the pedal off (fuzz behind a buffer:
  Premier Guitar, Morningstar, Screaminfx; the switchless wah staying on; power).
  No professional review of this model was found; the internal wah-off delay
  trim pot of older Morley switchless wahs is left out as unconfirmed for 20/20.
